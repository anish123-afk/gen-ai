Artificial Intelligence (AI) is one of the most important and rapidly developing areas of modern technology. It refers to the ability of computer systems and machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. These tasks include learning from data, understanding language, recognizing images, solving problems, making decisions, and generating content.

AI has become an important part of everyday life. It is used in smartphones, search engines, recommendation systems, healthcare, banking, education, transportation, entertainment, and many other fields. Recent developments in generative AI have further increased the importance and popularity of AI.
